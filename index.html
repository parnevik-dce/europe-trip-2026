Travel day Bergen Flåm / Oslo Munich Naples / Rome

The big picture

  • Norway (Aug 13–19): cool and rainy. Bergen, the fjord-crossing Norway-in-a-Nutshell route, then Oslo.
  • Germany (Aug 19–24): the relaxed middle. Munich as a base, with Alpine day trips (Zugspitze, Hohenschwangau castle). The only driving days.
  • Italy (Aug 24–30): hot and lively. Naples pizza + Pompeii, then four nights in Rome for the big sights.
  • Pace: arrival days are recovery-only, every city has a slow afternoon, and roughly half the dinners are grocery/cook-in nights.

Fri · Aug 14 — Full dayBryggen + Mount Fløyen
  • Morning: walk the colorful Bryggen wharf (UNESCO, ~1 hr)
  • Midday: Fløibanen funicular up Mt. Fløyen — trails at the top to roam. Buy tickets ahead: floyen.no/en/floibanen/buy-tickets
  • At the top: walk ~10–15 min to Lake Skomakerdiket, a quiet forest lake worth the stroll. (The lakeside café Skomakerstuen is weekends/holidays only, so it'll be closed on a Friday — skip it, the walk itself is the point.)

  • Afternoon: Fish Market (Fisketorget) — browse, grab a snack
  • Late afternoon: rest at the Airbnb
LunchFish & chips or a hot dog at the Fish Market — an experience, not a splurge.
DinnerEnhjørningen (the Unicorn) — historic Bryggen spot, simple Norwegian. Or grocery night.

Fri · Aug 21 — Alpine day tripZugspitze — Germany's highest peak (drive)
  • Drive to Garmisch-Partenkirchen (~1.5 hrs). Active-family Alpine day: cogwheel train + cable car to a 2,962m summit, snow in August, glacier, huge views.
  • Two ways up: German side from Garmisch (cogwheel train to glacier ~90 min, then cable car), or Austrian side cable car from Ehrwald (quicker, often cheaper). Bring passports for the Austrian ascent.
  • Budget most of the day. Layers + closed shoes — cold and windy up top even in August.
  • Weather matters: the summit clouds over often. Check the webcam that morning; if socked in, swap with Aug 22.
  • Back to Munich late afternoon — easy night.
LunchEat at the summit/glacier station, or pack sandwiches from a Munich bakery.
DinnerGrocery night, or a Bavarian Wirtshaus from the list above.
Sat · Aug 22 — Slow / flex dayRecover in Munich (or optional Salzburg)

  • Default — slow Munich day: sleep in, then Olympia Park (climb the Olympiaturm) + free BMW Welt where kids climb in the cars. Or the English Garden.
  • Optional — Salzburg, Austria: ~1.5 hrs each way. Old Town, Mozart's birthplace, Sound of Music. Bring passports. Skip the wider Berchtesgaden loop.
LunchMunich day: grocery picnic in Olympia Park. Salzburg day: eat in the Old Town.
